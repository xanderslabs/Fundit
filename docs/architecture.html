Fundit V2 on Arc mainnet

Fundit architecture

Every fundraiser is its own escrow contract on Arc. Donations arrive in USDC or EURC, the creator withdraws to Arc or to another CCTP chain, and no operator key can move the funds.

Pre-launch. The contracts are not deployed yet. Cross-chain donations, unified balance donations and passkeys are built and tested on an Arc mainnet fork, and stay behind build flags until a live canary confirms them.

Trust model

The owner can

  • Set the donation and withdrawal fee for new campaigns, each at most 5%. Launch values are 1% and 1%.
  • Rotate the treasury.
  • Set a featured campaign.
  • Pause the creation of new campaigns.

The owner can never

  • Move, freeze or redirect campaign funds.
  • Pause donations or withdrawals.
  • Change an existing campaign's fees, token or creator.
  • Upgrade any contract.

Creator recovery

  • An optional backup can start a recovery.
  • After 7 days (RECOVERY_DELAY) the backup becomes the creator.
  • The creator keeps every power during the wait and can cancel it.

EURC campaigns

EURC campaigns take donations and pay out on Arc only. On an Arc mainnet fork, depositForBurn(EURC) reverts with "Burn token not supported" for domains 0, 1, 6 and 14. The factory's EURC payout list is fixed at deploy and starts empty.
