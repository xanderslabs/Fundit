Fundit V2 on Arc mainnet

Fundit architecture

Every fundraiser is its own escrow contract on Arc. People sign in with a Fundit account, by email (a Circle user-controlled wallet) or with their own wallet. Donations arrive in USDC or EURC, the creator withdraws to Arc or to another CCTP chain, and no operator key can move a creator's balance.

Live on Arc mainnet. fundit.fun runs on Arc mainnet, and the factory and implementation are deployed. Email sign-in stays off on mainnet until the mainnet email check passes, and wallet sign-in is available. See Deployed contracts. The app has an Arc testnet build setting, and the end-to-end suite runs every money flow on Arc testnet against the testnet factory.

Trust model

The owner can

  • Set the donation and withdrawal fee for new campaigns, each at most 5%. Deployed values are 1% and 1%.
  • Set the treasury to any non-zero address. claimFees pays the treasury named at claim time, so a new treasury also receives unclaimed fees and tips from existing campaigns.
  • Set a featured campaign.
  • Pause the creation of new campaigns.

The owner can never

  • Move, freeze or redirect any creator balance.
  • Pause donations or withdrawals.
  • Change an existing campaign's fees, token or creator.
  • Upgrade any contract.
  • Give up ownership. renounceOwnership always reverts.

Creator recovery

  • An optional backup can start a recovery.
  • After 7 days (RECOVERY_DELAY) the backup becomes the creator.
  • The creator keeps every power during the wait and can cancel it.

EURC campaigns

EURC campaigns take donations and pay out on Arc only. On an Arc mainnet fork, depositForBurn(EURC) reverts with "Burn token not supported" for domains 0, 1, 6 and 14. The factory's EURC payout list is fixed at deploy and starts empty.
